# MacJournal roadmap

Implemented: local encrypted persistence, daily Markdown notes, app usage, normal-window browser metadata, rich title readers, incremental Chrome history import, four dashboard categories, search, and duration/visit separation.

Possible future work: opt-in retrospective history import, more browser adapters with reliable private-mode checks, a user-selected Obsidian vault, and local journal reflection. These are ideas, not implemented features. Screenshots and OCR capture are not part of the recorder.
